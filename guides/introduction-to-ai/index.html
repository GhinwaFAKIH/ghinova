Introduction to AI

Learn what AI is with familiar examples, simple pictures and a short quiz.

1

What is AI?

AI is software that can help with tasks such as recognizing patterns, suggesting words, or answering questions. It is not a person and can make mistakes.

2

Where do we see AI?

A phone suggesting the next word, a music app recommending songs, and a chatbot answering a question are everyday examples.

3

What is machine learning?

Instead of writing every rule by hand, developers can show a computer examples so it can learn patterns. It still needs testing.

4

Try it yourself

Think of three apps you use. Which ones make suggestions or predictions? What could go wrong if their suggestions are incorrect?
