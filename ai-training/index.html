Questions about online AI training

Can I learn AI without coding?

Yes. Start with the Introduction to AI lesson, which explains the basics without requiring programming experience.

Are there free AI courses?

GHINOVA offers free introductory lessons and downloadable workbooks. Full paid workshops and courses, where available, are listed separately in the shop.

Can I learn Python for AI?

Yes. The Python for AI Beginners lesson starts with simple examples and a small exercise.

Do I receive a certificate?

The free lessons do not currently provide a certificate or accredited qualification.
