Do I need coding experience?

No. Sessions are tailored to your current level, including complete beginners.

What can I learn?

Choose Python programming or AI topics such as generative AI, prompt engineering, AI agents, RAG and knowledge graphs. We will focus on your goals.

How do I book and pay?

Click the Book button, choose an available time on Cal.com and complete payment during checkout. Your session is confirmed through the booking process.

Are the prices before tax?

Yes. Listed training prices exclude tax. An additional 20% VAT is shown at checkout.

How do the 5-hour and 10-hour packages work?

Packages are €55 for five hours and €100 for ten hours, before tax. Please contact GHINOVA to arrange package payment and scheduling; the online booking button currently covers one hour.

Can I reschedule a session?

Use the rescheduling options in your booking confirmation if available, or contact GHINOVA to discuss changes.
